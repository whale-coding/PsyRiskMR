1. Overview


Psychiatric disorders represent an increasingly important global health problem that impose enormous clinical and economic burden on modern society. However, the etiology of psychiatric disorders is largely unknown, and no effective therapies are currently available. Therefore, it is crucial to identify the risk factors for psychiatric disorders, which promises to shed new light on the potential pathogenesis and therapeutic strategies of psychiatric disorders. Mendelian randomization (MR) has developed into an effective and powerful statistical method to investigate the potential causal risk factors of diseases.

Here, we developed an online comprehensive MR database, PsyRiskMR, to integrate and share risk factors for psychiatric disorders identified from our systematic and exhaustive two-sample MR analysis. PsyRiskMR contains various layers of risk factors for psychiatric disorders, including psychiatric disorders-specific risk phenotype, risk brain image, and bulk-tissue/cell-specific biomarker. Overall, the PsyRiskMR not only offered a user-friendly web interface for the research community to browse, search, and download all MR results for psychiatric disorders, but also provided new insights for better prevention and treatment of psychiatric disorders.

3. Data resources


3.1 The outcome GWASs selection for 10 psychiatric disorders

The GWAS summary data was used for constructing IVs in MR analysis. We downloaded the largest available GWAS summary statistics for MR analysis of ten common psychiatric disorders that were publicly available from Psychiatric Genomics Consortium (PGC, https://www.med.unc.edu/pgc), including attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), Alzheimer’s disease (ALZ), anxiety disorder (ANX), autism spectrum disorder (ASD), bipolar disorder (BP), eating disorders (ED), major depressive disorder (DEP), obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD), schizophrenia (SCZ). In addition, all of the GWASs incorporated in this study were based on individuals of European ancestry. Detailed information regarding the summary statistics can be accessed from the supplementary materials and the primary research publications.

3.3 Risk brain imaging datasets

We acquired the processed Genome-Wide Association Study (GWAS) summary statistics comprising 3,935 brain imaging traits as reported by Smith et al. in this study. The heritable imaging-derived phenotype (IDP) generated from UK Biobank were grouped into 17 categories and all of these categories have been included in our study. The full GWAS summary statistics were downloaded from BIG40 (Oxford Brain Imaging Genetics Server, available at https://open.win.ox.ac.uk/ukbiobank/big40/). 4. Database usage


PsyRiskMR provides users with a quick overview of the significant MR summary statistics and detailed MR results for each risk factors and diseases. Considering the speed and the burden of web server, PsyRiskMR does not directly display all of the MR statistics online, users can conveniently get all the of the detailed results through the download interface instead.

4.1 Search all MR results by psychiatric disorders


The search interface allows users to visually search by ten psychiatric disorder categories. At the same time, Risk categories of interest can also be searched, including Risk phenotype, Risk brain imaging, Bulk-tissue biomarker, and Cell-specific biomarker. Of course, you can also enter Risk factors, such as gene names, directly into the Risk factor box for search. This search page can be visually and systematically displayed to researchers, and users can download all the MR Analysis results within the threshold.


Search all MR results module

4.2 Risk phenotype and brain imaging


The construction of Risk phenotype and brain imaging modules are quite similar. These module included Sankey diagrams of significant results and partial MR results for online display. These modules have facilitated researchers in gaining a deeper comprehension and in the identification of risk factors linked to specific psychiatric disorders. In addition, users could retrieve interested information through the "Search" interface, sort the results, and download complete dataset of MR analysis.


Risk phenotype and brain imaging module

4.3 Bulk-tissue biomarker and cell-specific biomarker


The modules for Bulk-tissue biomarker and Cell-specific biomarker were similar. It contained a navigation page with hyperlinks to the subpage of different psychiatric disorders. These modules provided researchers a better understanding of and identifying biomarkers associated with specific psychiatric disorders. Moreover, users could access their required genes with the "Search" interface, sort the information they wanted, and download the entire dataset of MR analysis.

5. Web Browser Requirements


The PsyRiskMR requires a modern web browser with JavaScript and cookies enabled. To view the complex details, pop-ups must not be blocked. The following browsers have been thoroughly tested with PsyRiskMR:
• Mozilla Firefox, version 4 or above
• Internet Explorer, versions 9 or above
• Chrome, version 5 or above
• The latest version of Firefox and Chrome is recommended for visualization.
